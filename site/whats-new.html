What’s new

What each release of Omabook brought, newest first. The same list is a feed.

0.5.0 2026-11-23

Omabook gets a hub: a computer that is always on keeps your libraries without a window, linked to your other computers over your own ssh by a key that opens nothing but Omabook, and synced with them from the Hubs panel. The library has snapshots to go back to, made with restic: every night on a hub and just before anything that changes much, copied to a second disk and to a place away from home, each showing what changed since, with a book, a notebook or the whole library put back; a backup that is old, failed or missing warns. This upgrade takes a moment snapshot first, where snapshots are set up, and then repairs, once, the books a computer got by a sync with an earlier Omabook, so a change made to them, and their details, reach your other computers again; a sync is now checked on both computers before it writes. The package now needs `restic`, suggests `rclone`, and brings the hub's systemd units.

0.4.1 2026-11-22

Timelines branch, for alternate history and the worlds that go another way. Phones and e-readers connect by a QR code, and the library is served with the window closed too. Each use of AI can fall back to a second server when its own can't be reached, and finds a model on Ollama's library or Hugging Face; a book's graph builds again with a model on Ollama. The package suggests `tailscale`, for the address by this computer's name on the tailnet.

0.4.0 2026-11-22

Omabook listens: notes dictated, questions asked by voice and answered aloud, what to do said while reading, and recordings written out to read and search. Ink goes in the margins and between the lines of every book, the margin folds away to dots, and each row of the AI page can be tested and finds a missing model. The package now needs `qt6-websockets`, and `ffmpeg` for recordings in other formats than WAV and Opus.

0.3.2 2026-11-22

0.3.1's one-time library upgrade could take a book's own identifiers away; this one keeps them. Upgrade to 0.3.2, not 0.3.1.
