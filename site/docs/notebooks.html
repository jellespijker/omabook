Documentation / Notebooks

Notebooks

Notebooks keeps notebooks of pages on paper beside the books you read: lined, squared, dotted, blank, music staves or Cornell.

Install

Install it from Find plugins (I in the plugin manager), or in a terminal with omabook plugin add notebooks. It comes from jellespijker/omabook-notebooks (MIT), asks for files, microphone and clipboard permissions, and needs Omabook 0.2.0 or newer.

Pages, ink and recordings

Write with a pen, type, add pictures and recordings. Handwriting is read, recordings are transcribed, and a page links to the passage you were reading. Export as PDF or Markdown.

Private by default

Notebooks save in the .omanotebook format and are private books: they stay in your own collections and on your own computers unless you let one go. See Plugins for managing plugins.
