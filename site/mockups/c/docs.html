AI setup, per capability

Omabook asks a model to do six different kinds of work. Each has its own provider and model, and its own say on whether a hosted one may be used. Open Settings with , and go to the AI page.

Choose a provider for each row

CapabilityWhat it does
AnswersThe reading companion, catch-ups, who is who, asking about your library.
Quick workBuilds the knowledge graph, tidies the library, judges look-ups, understands voice commands the grammar doesn’t know.
HandwritingReads ink in margins and notebooks. A server of its own if you want one.
Speech to textDictation and recordings written out. voxtype first, then whisper.cpp, then a server.
Text to speechSpoken answers and read-aloud, by the voice you choose.
EmbeddingsSearch by meaning across the library.

Start local. On the AI page, pick Ollama for a row and leave the address as http://localhost:11434/v1. Ollama on another computer is the same row with another address.

Answers          Ollama   llama3.2           localhost:11434
Quick work       Ollama   qwen2.5:7b         localhost:11434
Handwriting      Ollama   qwen2.5vl:3b       localhost:11434
Speech to text   voxtype
Text to speech   Piper
Embeddings       Ollama   nomic-embed-text   localhost:11434

Example values. Your rows will name your own models.

Press Test

Every row has a Test button, or T on the row. It sends one small real request through exactly that row’s provider, server, key and model: a two-word reply, a tiny drawn word read back, a short clip heard. If the model is missing, Omabook finds one that can do the job on the server, or offers a small known one to download with one press.

Allow hosted, one row at a time

A hosted provider is used only for a capability that allows it. Turn a row’s hosted switch off and nothing for that capability leaves your computer. With AI off, nothing is asked and nothing is listened to.
