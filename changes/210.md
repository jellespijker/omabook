section: Fixed
---

- A change to a book's details on a computer that got the book from a sync now reaches the other computer (#210). Before, a new title, authors, ISBN, series, description, publisher or tags made there stayed there, for books on paper and books with files alike, and a cleared field never reached the sync's check (`--accept`) either. A title changed there could even look to the other computer like a book with no author, description or ISBN, and the sync was refused.
- A book a computer got from a sync now goes on whole to a third computer (#210): one on paper no longer fails with "A book needs a title", and one with a file no longer arrives without its title and details.
