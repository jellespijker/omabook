- Fold the margin notes away (#167): Shift+E, the small button at the margin's edge, or the Aa panel folds the margin column away for every book, kept across books and restarts, and the text takes its room, which with large type is real width. A dot beside each line with a margin note, typed or handwritten, someone else's note or ink in the margin stands for them, in the note's colour, one dot with a count where a line has several; a click, a finger's tap or the pen opens the line's note on its card beside the words, to read, edit or write on, and Escape or a tap elsewhere closes it, the margin still folded. Tab goes through the dots and Enter opens one, and a screen reader names each by its note's first words. Where the window has no room for the margin column at all, the same dots stand beside the lines.
