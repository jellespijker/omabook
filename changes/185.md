- A Snapshots panel (Shift+B, the library's menu, or the settings): what is wrong with the snapshots first, in red, with what to do; where they are kept and the newest in each place; **Snapshot Now**; the list of them with a filter, a moment snapshot named by what it came before. Each shows what changed since, against now or another snapshot: the books added, removed and changed, detail by detail, and the annotations and the knowledge graph counted. A book changed since is put back as the snapshot has it, its details or its annotations, after showing what that changes and asking; a moment snapshot is taken first, and Undo goes back to it. The whole library is restored into a new folder only, never over the one in use, and can be opened from there. Snapshots not set up yet are set up here, the recovery key shown once; destinations are added (a second disk, or a place away from home with its credentials), tested and taken off.
