section: Fixed
---

- Tidy no longer drops what only the folded copy of a book had: its description, tags, publisher, ISBN, series and other details move to the copy that is kept. Where the two copies say different things, tidy names what would not be kept, leaves the pair unticked, and `tidy --apply` leaves it for you to pick by its number.
- The window's Tidy takes a moment snapshot first where snapshots are set up, and changes nothing when none can be taken. A copy with no file is no longer listed as "()".
