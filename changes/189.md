- The Hubs panel (#189), from the settings, the library's menu and Shift+Y: each hub this computer is linked to, whether it can be reached, its Omabook, its libraries, the computers linked to it, and what is wrong with its snapshots, in red, with what to do. Link a hub by saying where it is, then yes to what linking does there: it signs in once over your own ssh (Omabook never asks for a password, nor sends one) and installs a key of this computer's own that opens only the hub's session. Unlink takes the key off again, and the hub's other computers can be taken off it, after asking. Sync Now syncs the library with the hub (#188), and Preview first says what a sync would do, changing nothing. Keyboard, mouse and touch.
- `omabook hub forget <host> <computer>` takes another of your computers off the hub from this one, through the hub's session.
