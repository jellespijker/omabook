- A backup is never old unnoticed: `omabook snapshot status` says, for each place the snapshots are kept, when the newest was taken and when they were last checked, and warns, saying what to do, when the newest is over two days old, a nightly snapshot or a check failed, their password is missing, a second disk isn't there or a disk is nearly full, or three copies on two kinds of medium with one away from home no longer hold. `omabook snapshot check` has restic check them (with `--read-data`, reading them back too); a warning that comes is shown as a desktop notification, once, and again a week on while it stands (#184).
- The hub checks its snapshots every week and reads them back every month (all of a disk's, a twelfth of a place away's), says each new warning in its journal, and `omabook hub status` shows them (#184).
