- More than one hub: a computer linked to several syncs with each through the sync guard, and the hubs don't fight. The same library on two hubs ends the same, what automatic sync brings from one hub goes on to the others (and never back), a hub out of reach holds up none of the others, and each is tried again as the network comes back.
- A book's graph is built once with several hubs: the first hub to take a book up claims it in the library, and another hub that learns of the claim leaves the book to it and gets the graph by the sync.
- Another of your hubs as a destination of the snapshots: `omabook snapshot destination add <name> hub:<host>`, reached over the session with that hub, never a shell. It keeps them encrypted and can't read them, the checks reach it, and it counts as the copy away from home.
