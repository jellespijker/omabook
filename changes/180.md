- Link a computer to its person's hub (#180): `omabook hub add <host>` makes the computer a key of its own and installs it on the hub over your ssh, where it opens nothing but Omabook's hub session, never a shell, a terminal or a forwarded port. Only the hub owner's computers are taken: the first one linked names its owner (`omabook identity`). `omabook hub remove <host>` takes the key off again, `omabook hub computers [<host>]` lists the hub's computers, and `omabook hub status <host>` asks the hub what it keeps. On the hub, `omabook hub forget <computer>` takes one's key away. No password is ever asked for or sent.
