- A sync is checked before anything is written (#187): it is planned on both computers first, and refused, with nothing changed on either, when it would lose a book the other still has, empty a field that had a value (a title, an author, an ISBN, a description, a tag, a shelf, stars, a cover), remove an identifier the dry run doesn't announce or one that is the book's own, give a book paired by an identifier another book's title, authors, ISBN or identifiers without a rename agreed to, take a reading place read earlier than the one it has, or move a bookmark to another book. It names the books and why; `omabook sync <computer> --accept "<title>"` lets one book's change through, and `--dry-run` lists the same. After a sync both computers are compared with what it announced, and anything else it did is said.
