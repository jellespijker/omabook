- The hub builds the knowledge graph of each new book in the background (#192), whole, on its schedule, one book at a time, with the quick model of its own settings (`config.json` in the hub's state folder, as a computer's is; it starts local, and nothing goes to someone else's computer unless hosted is allowed there for quick work). The graph reaches your computers by the normal sync, placed where each thing was learned, so nothing past your place shows. `omabook hub status` and the Hubs panel say which book it is working on and how far it got, and `omabook hub cancel` or the panel's Cancel Work stops it, keeping nothing of that book's job. A job done is never done again; one that failed is tried again an hour on.
