section: Added

- A library restored whole from a snapshot (`omabook snapshot restore <snapshot> --to <folder>`) is given a device id of its own, and says so: it syncs as a computer of its own, never as the one it came from (#209).
- A sync refuses, before it writes anything on either computer, a library with this library's device id, or with the device id of another of your computers: a copy of one. It says what to do: `omabook identity --new-device` gives a copy a device id of its own (#209).
- `omabook snapshot restore <snapshot> --notebook <title or id>` puts a notebook of the Notebooks plugin back as the snapshot has it, with its pages and what is on them, and `--page <id>` one page: shown first and asked, with a moment snapshot to undo it with. Pages, strokes and text removed or changed since come back; what was made since stays (#209).
