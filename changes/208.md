- A library brought up to date by a newer Omabook takes a moment snapshot first, where snapshots are set up, and is left as it was when none can be taken, on a hub and on a computer alike; without snapshots it is brought up to date with its copy in `.backups`, as before, and says so (#208).
- An agent's change to more than 10 books at once, through the MCP tools (`tidy_apply`, `collection_put`, `collections_sort_apply`) or the commands it runs (`collections include`, `exclude` or `follow`, `collections sort --apply`, `embed`), takes a moment snapshot first, and the tool's answer gives its id as the undo; one that can't be taken is noted, and the change goes on (#208).
