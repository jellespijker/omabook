- Snapshots of the library, made with restic: encrypted, each keeping only what changed, the database copied whole while no one writes, with the books, the settings and the plugins' data. `omabook snapshot setup` makes the place for them and shows the recovery key once; `omabook snapshot now` takes one, `omabook snapshot list` lists them, and `omabook snapshot nightly` takes the night's and thins the older ones (every one of 14 days, then 7 daily, 4 weekly, 12 monthly, and one of every year). A sync, an import or a tidy that changes something takes a moment snapshot first, when something changed since the last one (#181).
