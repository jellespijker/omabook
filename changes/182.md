- Snapshots go to a second disk and to a place away from home too: `omabook snapshot destination add <name> <where>` makes a restic repository there (a folder on another disk, or sftp:, rest:, s3:, b2:, rclone:) with a recovery key of its own, and the snapshots are copied there every night and thinned as they are at home, by `omabook snapshot nightly` or the hub's daemon. A disk that isn't plugged in is said, and the others are copied all the same; `list` says how each last copy went and whether three copies, on two kinds of medium, one away from home, hold. What a place needs to be reached (`--credential`) is kept as a secret, never with the choices (#182).
