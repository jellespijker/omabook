- Recordings written out (#151): an MP3, M4B, M4A, Ogg, Opus, FLAC or WAV added to the library is a book once it is written out through speech to text, as chosen on the AI page (voxtype first, a hosted way only where it is allowed, nothing with the AI Off): Write Out in its details, or Shift+E, writes it out in the background five minutes at a time, shown at the top of the window with a Stop. Stopped, it goes on from where it was; written out, it reads as its transcript in chapters of ten minutes, and the shelf's search finds it by what is said in it. Plugins write out their own recordings in one call (`omabook.recognize.transcript`), kept and resumed the same way.
