- The library diff: `omabook snapshot diff <snapshot> [<snapshot>]` tells what changed in the library from one snapshot to another, or to now, as you see the library: books added and removed, a book's details before and after (title, authors, ISBN, series, description, publisher, identifiers...), and how many notes, highlights, bookmarks and ink pages and things of the knowledge graph came, went or changed. With `--json` for a program (#183).
- Going back to a snapshot: `omabook snapshot restore <snapshot> --to <folder>` restores the whole library into a new folder, never over the one in use; `--book <title or id>` puts one book's details or annotations (`--details`, `--annotations`) back into the library, after showing what changes and asking, with a moment snapshot first to undo it with. Annotations and ink strokes made since stay. A hub does the same through its one writer (#183).
