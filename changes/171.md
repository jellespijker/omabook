- Timelines that branch (#171): a timeline can branch off another at an event, the same up to it and its own after it. Alternate history is a branch of real history, so a novel in which the war ends otherwise no longer mixes its events in with real history; an alternative universe, a reboot or a Storyteller story's branch is a branch of its world's or its story's timeline, and a branch can branch again. The events before the split show on both, and the chronology draws the branch beside its parent from the split on, the split marked and named, never before you have read it. When the AI reads an alternate history or a world that goes otherwise, it only proposes the branch: the chronology asks you to take it or not, and until then its events stay in their book's own order. By hand, `omabook chronology timeline branch <name> --from <timeline> --at <event>` and `timeline accept|refuse <branch>`, and over MCP `chronology_timeline_branch` and `chronology_timeline_decide`; plugins have `omabook.graph.timeline` and `omabook.graph.branch`. Branches go to your other computers and into spaces like the rest of the chronology.
